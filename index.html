ABOUT THE EXPERIMENT

Music, rendered as matter.

CHLADNI maps live audio to an ideal circular aluminum plate. Several prominent spectral peaks excite a weighted mix of plate modes; grains are driven by the vibrating surface and settle toward low-displacement nodal regions. The fixed model uses a 200 mm diameter, 1 mm thickness, and a free outer rim.

YouTube playback provides timing but not raw spectrum. For live spectral response, choose this tab and share its audio when prompted. The browser analyzes the shared tab audio locally.
